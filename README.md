# Spectral extensions of Vinay (2019) and Wicklin (2021): resolving Spezia's conjectures in OEIS A322844, COMMENTS, 28 December 2018 and 6 January 2019

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

For the conjectures in OEIS A322844, COMMENTS, first posed on 28 December 2018 and 6 January 2019, we extend the arithmetic-row rank argument of Vinay (2019) and Wicklin (2021) to an explicit spectral formula. For the n by n row-serpentine matrix, the characteristic polynomial is x^(n-2)(x^2-T_n*x-A_n), with T_n=n(n^2+(n mod 2))/2 and A_n=n^2[3(1+n^2)-2(2+n^2)(n mod 2)]/12. Thus the signed second exterior trace is -A_n and all higher exterior traces vanish. Written corollaries give spectra, arbitrary row directions, linked sum and permanent formulas, generating functions, reciprocal sums, the A246696 permutation property and corrected even-row odd count, and first off-diagonal parity and terminal-digit statements. General rank antecedents and the earlier even-order sum proofs of De Los Reyes et al. (2007) are credited. The relation of the unavailable 2012 Miranda construction articles to the explicit spectral coefficient remains unresolved; no priority claim is made.
